# Account-native expression

Trigger: one of the two Producer identities receives its released role-scoped Task.

Inputs: frozen binding digest, assigned ActivationUnits, CapabilitySnapshots and content-language brief. Outputs: immutable schema-valid ArtifactRevision submissions for only the assigned platforms.

Founder Producer scope: X and Xiaohongshu. Product Producer scope: Bluesky and LinkedIn. Allowed tools: task lifecycle, evidence read, configured ModelProvider, safe trace append. Failure: reject unassigned platform, unsupported field, constraint overflow, schema or digest mismatch, model timeout/error. Privacy: secrets are gateway-only and model snapshots are redacted. Permission: produce only; no audit, Owner approval, Grant or action.

Tests: platform scope, field limits, immutable digest, malformed model output, Beta-to-GA fault and corrected revision.
