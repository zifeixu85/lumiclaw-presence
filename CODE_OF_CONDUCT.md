# Community Code of Conduct

LumiClaw Presence aims to be a constructive, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good intent while remaining honest about risk.
- Respect privacy, consent, platform rules, and the boundaries of other people's accounts and data.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant affiliations or conflicts.
- Help newcomers understand the project's contracts and claim-maturity discipline.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, credential or private-data disclosure, and attempts to bypass safety or platform policy are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report community incidents privately through the repository's security reporting channel when confidentiality is needed, or contact the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions will consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
