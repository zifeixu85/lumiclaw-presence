# SDD-005 Constitution

1. Customer market material never enters public fixtures, prompts, logs or commits.
2. Actionable localization knowledge requires provenance, scope, version and review time.
3. Model prior is a proposal source, not approved market truth.
4. Market, locale, content language, platform and time zone remain separate.
5. Public packs are synthetic engineering fixtures and do not prove cultural, legal or business outcomes.
6. Context is selected per ActivationUnit and projected by role; no unrelated-market bulk prompt.
7. Producer and Auditor remain independent; neither approves an external action.
8. Public pack or Organization knowledge changes never silently rewrite approved artifacts.
9. No crawling, credentials, connector, ActionGrant, external action or customer upload pipeline is authorized.
10. Only Coordinator may update canonical status; Executor returns evidence and a structured handoff.
